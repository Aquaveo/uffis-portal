About UFFIS

The Urban Flood Forecasting Information System runs flash flood forecasts every hour for five national meteorological and hydrological services, and gives each service its own portal.

  1. Every hour

    EF5 routes satellite rainfall, a nowcast and an ensemble forecast through each country's catchments.

  2. Forecasts and impact

    Streamflow, soil saturation, flood probability and impact on buildings, roads and people are published openly. This map reads them directly.

  3. A portal for each service

    Each service briefs and warns from its own portal, with its own accounts and data.
